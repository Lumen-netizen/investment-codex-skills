# Pass Decision Memo

Use for a conclusion that a new purchase is not supported at the valuation date. Scale length to evidence and the user request; no minimum page count. Use a cover and contents when helpful for the document length.

## 1. Executive Decision
State Pass, security, current price/date and horizon. Explain the decisive reason: excessive price, inadequate return for risk, broken business thesis, or insufficient evidence. Distinguish a provisional judgment from a supported negative conclusion. Pass does not imply a sale or short recommendation.

## 2. Investment Case and Decisive Evidence
Briefly state the credible positive case, then the specific evidence or unsupported assumption preventing purchase. Cover business economics, management, financial quality or financing only to the depth needed to assess that issue. Present material counterevidence and explain the adopted judgment.

## 3. Relevant Valuation and Expectations
Apply the current handbook through SKILL.md. When quantifiable, show the appropriate method, material inputs, per-share reconciliation, conditional range and assumptions required by today's price. Explain why the available upside does not compensate for downside or uncertainty. Label today's value and its valuation date separately from any future target price/date; a discount to present value is not a holding-period return. Show the return horizon and distributions if projecting investment returns. Separate observed consensus from reverse valuation. For decisive non-price issues or missing inputs, provide the analysis actually supported and identify what cannot be valued; do not manufacture targets or probabilities.

## 4. Risks and Uncertainty
Explain potential permanent loss, capital/financing constraints and important alternative outcomes. Include the risk that the Pass judgment is wrong and the strongest evidence in favor of the positive case.

## 5. Reconsideration Conditions
State what price under specified assumptions, operating evidence, financing change or resolved data gap would justify revisiting. Use observable conditions and relevant timeframes, not generic promises to monitor. A lower price need not cure a governance or solvency issue.

## 6. Sources and Limitations
Attribute material facts and valuation inputs with dates. Disclose missing evidence, methodological limits and the handbook version used. Use only charts that help establish the decision.
