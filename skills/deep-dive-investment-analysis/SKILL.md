## Research Workflow

Adapt depth and order to the actual investment question.

1. **Gather dated evidence.** Prefer structured financial connectors when available and primary filings, earnings releases, transcripts and company announcements. Verify current prices and other time-sensitive claims through available data tools or browsing. Historical research uses only information available at the cutoff. Separate verified facts, management guidance, external forecasts and research assumptions; cite material claims and resolve conflicting definitions explicitly.
2. **Explain the economics.** Identify how the company earns and retains profits, industry changes, competition, development stage and the few variables driving value. Examine management incentives, governance, earnings quality, capital allocation and financing where they affect the thesis. Connect findings to cash flows, duration, capital needs or shareholder claims.
3. **Apply the handbook.** Follow the causal chain from economic attributes and operating drivers through normalized earnings, competitive duration and reinvestment to per-share value. Choose or construct a suitable valuation method. Build coherent operating, capital, financing and dilution scenarios at a common valuation date. Explain the value range, capital-to-equity bridge, dominant sensitivities and limitations. Do not require DCF, a second model, fixed margins of safety or a predetermined number of scenarios when these add no insight.
4. **Compare with price and examine counterevidence.** Distinguish observed analyst consensus, price-implied assumptions and this research's hypothesis. Show what is priced in and how any supported difference reaches per-share value. Accept fair pricing or no identifiable edge. Test fragile assumptions against the strongest relevant counterevidence and alternative explanations; describe residual uncertainty and observable facts that would change the model or conclusion.
5. **Reach a Buy / Pass conclusion.** Buy requires an evidence-supported case that the current price offers attractive prospective returns for the stated horizon given uncertainty, capital demands and downside. Explain the margin of safety and its assumptions; a good business or an unrefuted thesis alone is insufficient. Pass means no supported new purchase now: distinguish excessive price, inadequate return for risk, a broken thesis and insufficient evidence. Give reconsideration conditions. Pass does not itself mean sell, short, or a worthless business. For an existing holding, address the user's actual hold/add/reduce question without mechanically mapping Pass to liquidation.
6. **Deliver the finished report.** Use the Buy or Pass structure below, scaled to scope, and create the report file without a separate creation confirmation. Use conversation for progress, necessary clarification and discussion; a chat-only answer does not complete this skill unless the user explicitly changes the deliverable. Requested screening can stop once a decisive issue is supported, without a fabricated full valuation. Where evidence cannot support valuation, present conditional analysis, critical unknowns and a provisional Pass instead of false precision.

## Before Delivery

Check that the handbook method actually informed the analysis; facts and assumptions are distinguishable; dates, units, share counts and claims are consistent; growth pays for reinvestment; and value is not counted twice. Verify model arithmetic and the bridge to per-share value; reuse recorded checks for unchanged inputs and calculations, and rerun affected checks after changes. Check that price-implied expectations are not mislabeled as observed consensus, downside and uncertainty affect the verdict, and the important thesis-reversal conditions are explicit. Keep unresolved data gaps visible and report only validation actually performed.
